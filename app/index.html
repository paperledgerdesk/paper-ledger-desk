Help

Data — stored in this browser only. Export a JSON backup regularly. Clearing browser data deletes the ledger.

Money — totals use integer minor units for rounding. Different currencies are never added together.

Forecasts — estimates based on due dates, promises, and expenses you enter. Not predictions.

Reminders — the app opens a prefilled email or WhatsApp message. You press send; nothing is sent automatically.

Payments — paste your own payment link. No payment is processed here.

Integrity — review-only check. It does not change records.

Schema . Single-file, local-first.

Backups

Automatic snapshots are kept in this browser (up to the last 10, fewer when your data is large). They do not survive clearing browser data — export a backup file too.

Collection effectiveness

Based on recorded payment dates versus due dates. Small samples are not reliable predictions. Estimates only.

Financial health
Bank statement import (CSV only)

This is not a live bank feed. Export a CSV from your bank, then import it here. Columns needed: Date and Amount. Optional: Description, Reference, or Memo. Duplicate transactions (same date, amount, and description) are automatically skipped. Similar amounts are suggested as fuzzy matches. You confirm every match. No credentials are stored.
