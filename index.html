What it does

PDF invoices, quotes and statements

Download real PDFs with your logo, address, tax number and bank details. It says "TAX INVOICE" when you are VAT registered.

Send by email or WhatsApp

One tap opens a ready-written email or WhatsApp message. You attach the downloaded PDF yourself, and nothing is sent without you.

Friendly payment reminders

A reminder schedule (3 days before, on the due date, then 7, 14 and 30 days late) with gentle, direct or final wording.

A "Today" list

See which invoices need a nudge first, what is due this week and what came in.

Six currencies, never mixed

Invoice in ZAR, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD or CAD and pick your default currency. VAT or sales tax at your own rate. Totals stay separate per currency and are never added together.

Bank CSV import and matching

Import your bank statement CSV and match payments to invoices. Partial payments, overpayments and write-offs are handled.

Cash flow and insights

A cash forecast, aging buckets, money recovered from late invoices, collection rate by month and average days-to-pay per client.

Clients and recurring invoices

Client records with terms and VAT numbers. Recurring invoices create next month's draft for you to check and finalise.

Your data stays with you

The app makes no network requests. Data lives in your browser, with automatic snapshots and a one-click backup file you can keep safe.

Works on your phone

Built for small screens with large buttons and bottom navigation. It can be added to your home screen in supported browsers.
